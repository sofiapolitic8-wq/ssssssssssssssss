# DAVID.IO Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-18_

DAVID.IO is a game client. This page explains what the app does with data.

## What stays on your phone
Skins, controls layout, performance preset and other settings are stored locally on your device.

## What goes over the network
- **Game servers:** the game connects to the arena server you choose. Your nickname and skin are sent so other players can see you.
- **Team mode (optional):** if you create or join a team, your nickname and team chat messages are sent to the team server configured in the app.
- **Updates and notices (optional):** only if you configure your own update/API service in a build.

## What we do not do
- No ads and no sale of personal data.
- No location access. Microphone and camera permissions are requested only for features that need them (voice, Trails) and only when you use those features.
